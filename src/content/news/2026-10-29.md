- [KV Cache被压成常数！OCR开源新王：页数没上限，缓存砍96%](https://www.qbitai.com/2026/10/505773.html) `cn:qbitai`

- [OpenAI expands partnership with Oracle to bring its apps to ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/oracle-apps-in-chatgpt) `blog:openai-news`

## 8. Anthropic被曝用非法获取书籍训练Claude

## 9. Anthropic与美国GSA合作将Claude接入政务场景

Anthropic宣布扩大与美国总务管理局（GSA）的合作，将Claude大模型的能力覆盖到美国各级政府的政务服务场景，推动政务数字化升级。

- [Expanding our partnership with GSA to bring Claude to all levels of government](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-partnership-gsa) `hn · 48pts`

## 10. DeepMind发布Genie 4实时交互仿真世界模型

- [We’re announcing Genie 4, our new world model for real-time interactive simulation.](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-genie-4/) `blog:deepmind`

## 11. Google启动AI绘制人脑图谱研究项目
