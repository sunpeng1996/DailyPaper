## 7. 谷歌发布Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash大模型

- [Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash: Fast and cost-effective with longer clips](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/introducing-gemini-omni-1-2-flash/) `blog:google-ai`

## 9. DeepSeek 284B可在64GB内存设备无GPU运行

- [Llama-modchip: 284B DeepSeek on 64GB RAM, no GPU](https://github.com/synchopate/llama-modchip) `hn · 475pts`

## 10. 高盛宣布停用面向内部员工的Claude Code服务
