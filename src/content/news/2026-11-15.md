## 3. Google搜索集成Gemini 4 Hydrogen完成升级

- [Google Search gets a major upgrade with Gemini 4 Hydrogen](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/gemini-4-hydrogen-search-upgrade/) `blog:google-ai`

## 4. Google发布Gemini Decisions API多模态服务

Google发布全新Gemini Decisions API，基于自研的Gemini Decision大模型打造，可提供快速、校准的多模态决策能力，面向企业级客户开放调用，覆盖企业决策、内容审核等多类场景。

- [Gemini Decisions API: Fast, calibrated multimodal decisions](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/) `blog:google-ai`

## 5. Anthropic升级Claude Code支持并行AI工作流

Anthropic宣布升级Claude Code Projects功能，新增并行AI worker支持，可同时调度多个AI实例协同完成编码任务，大幅提升复杂编码项目的开发效率，降低开发者使用门槛。

- [Anthropic upgrades Claude Code Projects to run parallel AI workers](https://claude.com/resources/articles/projects-redesigned) `hn · 99pts`

## 6. OpenAI解散机器人团队 通用模型接管控制

OpenAI宣布解散旗下专门的机器人研发团队，转而通过已有通用大模型直接支撑机器人的运行控制，这一调整意味着大模型的通用能力已足以覆盖机器人场景的核心需求。

- [OpenAI disbands its robotics team as its general models start running robots](https://fortune.com/2026/11/13/openai-disbands-robotics-team-as-its-general-models-start-running-robots/) `hn · 177pts`

## 7. Llama.cpp发布v1.0 原生支持macOS应用

- [Llama.cpp hits v1.0 with a native app for macOS](https://huggingface.co/blog/ggml-org/llama-cpp-v1) `hn · 26pts`

## 8. OpenAI斥资200万美元支持AI儿童安全研究

- [Supporting research on AI and children’s safety](https://openai.com/index/children-first-declaration) `blog:openai-news`

## 9. GitLab 19.5新增智能体开发全流程治理能力
